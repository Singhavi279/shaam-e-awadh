What the fee includes

  • Review of the submitted performance video.
  • Evaluation by the Shaam-e-Awadh editorial and jury panel.
  • Consideration for a main-stage performance slot.
  • Communication regarding application status.
  • Access to the Open Mic application process.
  • Festival entry ticket.

What the fee does not include

  • Guaranteed selection.
  • Guaranteed performance slot.
  • Travel, accommodation, meals or local transport.
  • Professional recording, editing or promotion.
  • Prize, cash award or future performance commitment.

Refund policy

The application fee is non-refundable once the application has been successfully submitted.

Selection process

Stage 1

Eligibility screening

The editorial or events team checks:

  • Age eligibility.
  • Complete submission.
  • Acceptable video length.
  • Correct category.
  • Content suitability.
  • Availability for the festival dates.
  • Duplicate or multiple entries.

Stage 2

Editorial longlist

The editorial and curation team prepares a longlist based on:

  • Originality.
  • Clarity of expression.
  • Cultural relevance.
  • Stage readiness.
  • Emotional or intellectual impact.
  • Diversity of voices, languages and formats.
  • Suitability for a live, family-oriented audience.

Stage 3

Jury review

An internal jury reviews the longlist and scores each entry independently.

Stage 4

Final selection

Up to 20 participants will be selected across the two days.

10

Day 1: Lucknow Ki Virasat

Poetry, storytelling, folk, classical and heritage-rooted work

10

Day 2: Lucknow Ka Kal

Contemporary music, spoken word, youth voices, comedy and new expression

Performance format

  1. Reporting and briefing

    30 minutes before slot

  2. Stage introduction

    1 minute

  3. Performance

    4–6 minutes

  4. Transition

    1 minute

  5. Total stage time

    6–8 minutes

The exact slot duration will be finalised after the full programme is locked.

Stage rules

  • One microphone and basic backing-track support, where required.
  • No live band unless specifically approved.
  • No fire, smoke, sharp objects, hazardous props or unapproved pyrotechnics.
  • No political campaigning, hate speech, obscenity, defamation or communally sensitive content.
  • No unapproved brand promotion or product placement.
  • Performers must follow stage-manager cues and time limits.
  • The organiser reserves the right to stop a performance that violates safety, content or time requirements.

Onboarding selected participants

Selected participants must complete the following:

  1. 1Confirm acceptance within the stated deadline.
  2. 2Submit final performance title and description.
  3. 3Submit final audio or backing track, if applicable.
  4. 4Submit technical requirements.
  5. 5Submit high-resolution photographs.
  6. 6Sign participant agreement.
  7. 7Attend briefing.
  8. 8Attend rehearsal, if required.
  9. 9Report at the assigned time on the performance day.

Content, recording and rights

Participants consent to:

  • Recording of their performance.
  • Use of performance clips, photographs and excerpts across Shaam-e-Awadh, Navbharat Times and Times Internet Languages Live platforms.
  • Editorial and promotional use, subject to the organiser’s content standards.
  • No guarantee of publication, broadcast or monetisation.
  • The organiser’s right to edit, withhold or remove content that violates editorial, legal or safety standards.

Guardrails

  • One application per applicant.
  • No application is considered submitted until payment is successful.
  • No guarantee of selection or performance slot.
  • No guarantee of media coverage, recording or prize.
  • No unapproved political, religious, obscene or inflammatory content.
  • No last-minute changes to performance content without approval.
  • No unapproved props, instruments, equipment or accompanying persons.
  • The organiser reserves the right to modify the format, slot duration or selection number based on programme and operational requirements.
